> * **Loresheet Specifics:** *High Clan*, *Low Clan*, *Succubus Club Copycat*, and *Descendant of Marconius* are strictly banned. *Vienna Zero* is heavily moderated. *Descendant of Dracula* is replaced by homebrew.
