# Time and Timeskips

The game follows a real-time progression for the most part, where 14 OOC (Out Of Character) days are roughly equivalent to one IC (In Character) night.&#x20;

However, to keep the story moving and allow for long-term projects, we utilize Time Skips.

### Bi-Weekly Structure & Flow of Time

* **Active Play**: The majority of the IC night is dedicated to active roleplay, scenes, and immediate interactions. The IC night begins on Monday at 09:00 PM _(GMT / London time)_ and lasts for 12 days, ending on Saturday at 09:00 PM.
* **Daytime & Wrap-up**: The 48 hours between sundown and sunrise (Saturday night to Monday night) can be used to play daytime scenes (for humans, ghouls, or Thin-bloods) or to wrap up ongoing scenes. No new scenes are to be started or played, unless specified by the server staff.
* **Time Extensions**: An extension might be granted if a scene needs it, but it must be the final scene. No changing locations or starting new scenes (including new hunts) is permitted during an extension. Those are granted by the staff only in very specific situations.
* **The "Waking Up" Mechanic**: Every other Monday (OOC), a new IC week begins. This is when you perform your `/awaken` command in Inconnu to start a new night and heal your Willpower according to the rules and check for hunger.
  * If you are at Hunger 5 and fail the wake-up rouse check, you must ping the staff.
  * Remember to always include all your rolls (including awakenings and hunger changes) in your posts.

### Time Skips

The IC date is always set to the current month in the real world. The last Monday of an OOC month begins the last IC night of that month.&#x20;

At the end of that night, a Time Skip of approximately a month occurs to keep the game aligned with modern dates.&#x20;

During this time, characters are assumed to be performing their "routine" unlife, like hunting, maintaining havens, and working on long-term goals, etc etc.

### _What happens during a Time Skip_?

* **Project Progress**: This is the primary time to progress Projects (such as researching Rituals, Alchemy, or building Influence, working towards a goal and so on).
  * _Note:_ Rolls for projects must be made with the same Hunger level your character went to sleep at. Hunger does not automatically reset. Read more on projects below.

{% hint style="info" %}
All new projects must be submitted in a ticket with large notice. Any project submitted after the Saturday announcement will be disregarded as they would be too late to be acted by the staff.
{% endhint %}

* **Hunting**: Characters are assumed to have hunted successfully to maintain their current Hunger level, unless they are attempting to lower it or look for specific Resonance. Characters can perform one single hunting roll over a time skip to lower their hunger.
* **Healing**: Characters can heal one single damage at no cost, over a time skip. This damage could be Health or Willpower, and it could be Superficial or Aggravated.
* **Blood Bonds**: Time skips do not change how bonds are calculated; bonds fade at the standard rate of one level per 2 IC nights (1 OOC month).
* **Resuming Play**: After you have completed your rolls, you may wake up and resume as normal in the new night when it starts as normal.

### Projects

Projects are long-term actions that cannot be completed in a single scene.

* Starting a Project: Open a ticket to discuss the goal with the staff.
* Rolls: Most projects require a series of rolls (e.g., Intelligence + Occult for research).
* Timeframes: A project might take one or more Time Skips to complete depending on the complexity and the number of successes required.
* Variety: No 2 projects are the same. What worked once might not work again or it could be different and there could be complications.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Help us help you**: It's extremely important that you have a clear idea of what project you want to pursue and **how** you want to do as such. It's not the STs role to define what moves your character and how they reach their goals. We help shape the story, but the story is for you to tell.
{% endhint %}

### Loresheets and Merits <a href="#p-rc_0c6938ff9be9feb5-65" id="p-rc_0c6938ff9be9feb5-65"></a>

Advantages used in-game will be "greyed out" until replenished. They replenish at a rate of one dot per IC night, remaining greyed out on the first night after usage.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Example**: \
Jack has 3 dots of Resources. He wants to make an expensive purchase that costs 2 dots of Resources. \
He can use his dots to pay for it, greying out 2 of those 3 dots. \
For the current night and the following night, he will only have 1 dot of Resources available. \
The second night after, he will replenish one of the dots, and the third dot will replenish on the third night.
{% endhint %}

We keep track of those used Advantages in the specific discord channel, so do not forget to make a note of it.

Most Loresheets and some Advantages have usage limits written with tabletop in mind.&#x20;

In our play-by-post format, these translate as follows:

* Once per session / Once per chapter: Once per IC night (two OOC weeks).
* Once per story: Once per timeskip (once an OOC month).
* Once per chronicle: Once per 12 IC nights (6 OOC months, and requires prior discussion with the ST team). Those are usually extremely powerful and gamebreaking and there is a good chance that they will not regenerate at all.
