# [cite_start]Descendant Of Dracula [cite: 1]
[cite_start]*(Bloodline; Koldunic Tzimisce only)* [cite: 2]

[cite_start]You are a direct descendant of the infamous Dracula, a powerful vampire whose dark legacy has echoed through the ages. [cite: 6] [cite_start]Beyond the embrace of the Kindred, your lineage carries with it a potent connection to the ancient Koldunic sorceries, a rare and feared form of blood magic that taps into the raw power of the earth itself. [cite: 7] [cite_start]As a scion of Dracula, you bear the weight of a name that is both revered and reviled in the supernatural world. [cite: 8] [cite_start]The shadows of your ancestor's deeds cast a long and imposing silhouette, and you must navigate the intricate web of intrigue and politics that comes with such a lineage. [cite: 9] [cite_start]Allies may seek to align with you, while rivals and Hunters will seek to exploit your connection to the infamous Voivode. [cite: 10]

[cite_start]The power of Koldun, flowing through your veins, grants you control over the very elements and forces of nature. [cite: 11] [cite_start]You can summon storms, command the earth to tremble, and draw power from the land itself. [cite: 12] But this ancient magic comes with its own price. [cite_start]The spirits of the land demand respect and balance, and should you overstep, the consequences can be dire. [cite: 13]

[cite_start]Embrace your birthright cautiously, for the legacy of Dracula is both a blessing and a curse. [cite: 14] [cite_start]The shadows of history loom large, and the choices you make will shape the destiny that awaits you in the eternal night. [cite: 15]

## [cite_start]LORE [cite: 16]

* [cite_start]**Draculean Command:** You gain an innate ability to exert authority over your underlings. [cite: 18] [cite_start]You gain an extra die in Leadership and Intimidation rolls when dealing with those who serve you, reflecting the powerful influence that flows from your bloodline. [cite: 19] [cite_start]This power serves as a reminder of your lineage, allowing you to command respect and instil a sense of loyalty in those who follow your lead. [cite: 20]

* [cite_start]**Koldunic Bastion:** Koldunic Bastion grants you an additional die when using Blood Sorcery for rituals focused on defending your land. [cite: 21] [cite_start]Rooted in the ancient Koldunic tradition, this power allows you to forge a formidable shield around your territory. [cite: 22] [cite_start]Drawing strength from the very earth, you can weave potent wards and barriers that repel intruders and fortify the boundaries of your domain. [cite: 23]

* [cite_start]**Shadow’s Companion:** Your connection to the land and its inhabitants bestows upon you an additional loyal ally through the power of Bond Famulus. [cite: 24] [cite_start]This creature, bound to you by blood and heritage, stands as a steadfast aide in your endeavours. [cite: 25] [cite_start]Together, you form a formidable force, united by a common purpose and an unbreakable connection to the legacy of your lineage. [cite: 26] [cite_start]You gain an extra die when dealing with your Famuli. [cite: 27]

* [cite_start]**Strigoi's Polymorph:** Your strong lineage grants you the ability to acquire the Protean discipline, as if it was an inherent aspect of your clan. [cite: 28] [cite_start]Furthermore, it allows you to delve into the secrets of Flashcraft, Vicissitude, and Horrid Form, treating them as if they were in clan amalgams for you. [cite: 29]

* [cite_start]**Dracula’s Bonebrand:** Dracula's Bonebrand is a weapon forged from the dark artistry of Vicissitude, created with the bones of the fallen and reinforced with the strength of Koldun. [cite: 30] [cite_start]Under the moon's pale glow, it gleams with an eerie radiance, reminiscent of darkened metal. [cite: 31] [cite_start]This sinister blade carries the weight of your lineage and is imbued with the power to inflict aggravated damage upon the foes who threaten your domain or land. [cite: 32] [cite_start]This weapon serves as both a symbol of your authority and a devastating tool to protect what is rightfully yours. [cite: 33] [cite_start]Only the crafter of such a weapon can wield it and if it is lost or broken, the owner of the blade will receive stains as if one their touchstone was destroyed, such is the link between the Descendant of Dracula and their weapon. [cite: 34]
