# Discipline and advantages

## Disciplines

Is it possible to learn the three in clan disciplines without any issue, by spending experience and having the correct resonance.

Blood sorcery cannot be obtained as one of the three starter disciplines of a Caitiff. Clans that have Blood Sorcery in clan can take new dots without a mentor or a library.

Everyone else needs one or the other to be able to level up the discipline. Rituals all need to be learnt through studying.

Learning Blood Sorcery gives access to a level one ritual as well. Same for Oblivion, but the necromancy side of the discipline uses the same rules as Blood Sorcery.

To learn a discipline that's not part of the character's in-clan spread, they need to drink the blood of a character that either has the discipline in clan, or has dots in the discipline.

For example, drinking from a Toreador (Auspex 3, Presence 2, Potence 2) gives access to Auspex, Presence, Celerity and Potence.

Amalgams follow special rules, read more here [amalgams.md](amalgams.md "mention").
