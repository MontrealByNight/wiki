## Notre Dame de Grace (NDG)
**General Hunting DC:** 6

* **Trinity Memorial Church**: The historic and imposing 93-year-old gray limestone church on Sherbrooke Street West was dedicated as a memorial to soldiers who died in World War I. After a fire in 2023, the interior was consumed in flames. It now belongs to a fund to repurpose the church. Gradually the building is being brought back to life, offering a safe space for people to gather. The sacristy is now a kitchen, however, it is intermittently closed for maintenance.
    * **Coordinates**: `45.4748, -73.6076`
* **Villa Maria High School**: A private, prestigious high school known for its strong academic reputation and traditional architecture. The campus includes a mix of historical buildings and modern facilities, with well-maintained gardens and athletic fields. It is a prominent educational institution in NDG, providing a range of academic and extracurricular programs for its students.   
    * **Coordinates**: `45.4795, -73.6163`
* **Bistro le Bourbon**: A cozy and casual bistro located in NDG, known for its laid-back atmosphere and French-inspired menu. The interior is decorated with warm, rustic touches, including wooden tables, soft lighting, and vintage-style décor. It’s a popular spot for locals to enjoy a relaxed meal.
    * **Coordinates**: `45.4720, -73.6200`
* **Our Lady of Grace**: A modest stone parish church with a simple steeple rising above the surrounding neighborhood. Its exterior is light grey, with arched stained glass windows and a small set of steps leading to heavy wooden doors. Inside, the long nave is lined with wooden pews, leading to a raised altar beneath a vaulted ceiling.
