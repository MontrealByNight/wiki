## Notre Dame de Grace (NDG)
**General Hunting DC:** 6

* **Trinity Memorial Church**: 
- History  
The historic and imposing 93-year-old gray limestone church on Sherbrooke Street West was dedicated as a memorial to soldiers who died in World War I. A stone slab at the entrance pays tribute to David Alwyn Forneri, a lieutenant with the 73rd battalion of the Royal Highlanders of Canada, “killed in action at Vimy Ridge, France, while gallantly leading his men, March 1st, 1917.”  
After many years of serving the Anglican Catholic community, it could no longer provide service due to a lack of attendance and difficulty to afford its massive costs. The parish could only keep the mason work polished, but the inside remained without maintenance.   

- Fire  
In 2023, a fire erupted in the church. The smell of smoke filled the air in Montreal as the inner part of the building was consumed in flames. The exterior was mostly unscathed, but the building’s interior bore the brunt of the damage. Therefore, workers hammered planks of wood and barricaded the doors and windows to prevent a future blaze while a private developer proceeded to convert the building into a public service hub.  

- Today  
There was a delay in renovating the place due to a change in the administration. It now belongs to a fund to repurpose the church. Gradually the building is being brought back to life, offering a safe space for people to gather, and join work meetings and general events. The sacristy is now a kitchen. However, it is intermittently closed for maintenance due to “lack of funding” or undisclosed reasons.
* **Villa Maria High School**: A private, prestigious high school known for its strong academic reputation and traditional architecture. The campus includes a mix of historical buildings and modern facilities, with well-maintained gardens and athletic fields. It is a prominent educational institution in NDG, providing a range of academic and extracurricular programs for its students.   
* **Bistro le Bourbon**: A cozy and casual bistro located in NDG, known for its laid-back atmosphere and French-inspired menu. The interior is decorated with warm, rustic touches, including wooden tables, soft lighting, and vintage-style décor. The bistro offers a selection of classic French dishes, such as croque-monsieur and escargots, alongside local wine and craft beer options. It’s a popular spot for locals to enjoy a relaxed meal with friends or family in a welcoming environment.  
* **Our Lady of Grace Catholic Church**: A modest stone parish church with a simple steeple rising above the surrounding neighborhood. Its exterior is light grey, with arched stained glass windows and a small set of steps leading to heavy wooden doors. Inside, the long nave is lined with wooden pews, leading to a raised altar beneath a vaulted ceiling. During the day, soft light filters through the stained glass, giving the space a calm, welcoming atmosphere. A small parish hall and rectory sit nearby, serving as the center of community activity.
